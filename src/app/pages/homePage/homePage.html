How it works

From complaint to resolution

Step 1

File a complaint

Describe the issue, set priority, and attach evidence photos or URLs.

Step 2

Admin assigns officer

Admin reviews and assigns the complaint to a qualified field officer.

Step 3

Officer investigates

Officer visits the site, updates status, and adds resolution notes.

Step 4

Issue resolved

Citizen gets notified. Complaint marked resolved with full audit trail.
